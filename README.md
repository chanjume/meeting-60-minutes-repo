`main.py` writes one line — the single sentence that could have replaced an entire pointless meeting — to `/output/entry.txt`.

> In 60 minutes we made exactly one decision: to book another 60 minutes.

71 characters (limit 200). The meeting's only output is a second meeting: the recursion is the joke, and after this line no one could justify holding the meeting at all.
